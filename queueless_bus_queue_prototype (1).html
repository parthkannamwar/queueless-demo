DEMO MODE
Simple rule: One tap sends one status. The prototype shows the report time, but it cannot verify whether a student report is true.
How the one-tap idea works
1. Choose your bus

110 is the left stop; 117 is the right stop.

2. Tap what you see

Bus arrived, queue long, or queue moving. No typing or dropdowns.

3. Keep reports honest

Show recent reports as community observations—not verified live tracking. In a real pilot, stale reports expire and repeated spam is limited.

Important: A report is useful information, not a boarding pass. It never gives anyone queue priority.

What makes the concept useful?

Less
Unnecessary time standing at the stop, if arrival estimates are reliable enough.
Shared
Students help update bus sightings and queue conditions for everyone.
Measured
Pilot can compare reported wait time before and after, instead of claiming unproven savings.

How a real pilot could work

1

Start with sightings

Use a QR code at the gate. Students report bus sightings and approximate queue size with a timestamp.

2

Build reliable estimates

Compare reports with actual arrivals for several days. Add official GPS data if the operator makes it available.

3

Measure the outcome

Record how long students physically wait, how accurate estimates are, and whether they miss fewer buses.

QueueLess is a student ideathon prototype. Starting queue counts are illustrative; reports are stored only in this page and reset on reload. It has no live bus GPS or server connection and does not reserve a place in a public boarding queue.
